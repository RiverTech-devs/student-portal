# Berean Hall (Bible) — Lessons to Review (59)

All 59 lessons are newly AI-authored. Each is a concept lesson (steps + worked example + misconceptions) with a factual scripture/knowledge multiple-choice generator, written to a broadly evangelical/AG survey level. Recommend a **teacher AND pastoral review** for doctrinal accuracy and tone, especially the Doctrine & Theology and Apologetics & Worldview strands.

### Apologetics & Worldview
 1. **Apologetics (Foundations)** _(grade 9-12, stage 4)_ — give basic reasoned answers to common questions about the Christian faith and support them with Scripture and evidence
 2. **Worldview Analysis** _(grade 8-12, stage 4)_ — describe what a worldview is, contrast a biblical worldview with other worldviews, and explain how core beliefs shape values and choices
 3. **Advanced Apologetics** _(grade 10-12, stage 5)_ — engage advanced objections to Christianity and construct a well-organized, evidence-based defense of the faith

### Bible Basics & Structure
 4. **Bible Structure** _(grade K-3, stage 1)_ — explain that the Bible is one book made of many books divided into the Old and New Testaments and state that it contains 66 books
 5. **Books of the Bible** _(grade K-3, stage 1)_ — name books of the Bible in order and identify whether a given book belongs to the Old or New Testament
 6. **Finding a Verse (Book, Chapter, Verse)** _(grade 1-3, stage 1)_ — read a Scripture reference and locate the correct book, chapter, and verse in a Bible
 7. **Bible Translations** _(grade 4-7, stage 2)_ — explain that the Bible was written in original languages and translated into English, and identify common translations (e.g., KJV, NIV, ESV)
 8. **Old & New Testament Divisions** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — sort Bible books into their major divisions (Law, History, Wisdom/Poetry, Prophets; Gospels, Acts, Epistles, Revelation) and describe what each division contains

### Bible Study Skills
 9. **Memory Verses** _(grade K-3, stage 1)_ — recite a memorized Bible verse together with its reference and explain in simple terms what it means
10. **Reading Scripture Independently** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — look up a passage on their own, read it aloud, and summarize what it says in their own words
11. **Understanding Context** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — identify who wrote a passage, to whom, and why, and explain how the surrounding verses affect its meaning
12. **Biblical Interpretation** _(grade 6-10, stage 4)_ — apply basic interpretation principles (genre, context, comparing Scripture with Scripture) to explain the intended meaning of a passage
13. **Biblical Languages (Intro)** _(grade 9-12, stage 4)_ — explain that the Old Testament was written mainly in Hebrew and the New Testament in Greek, and describe how word studies aid interpretation

### Christian Living & Discipleship
14. **Prayer** _(grade K-4, stage 1)_ — explain what prayer is, describe different kinds of prayer (praise, thanks, asking, confession), and pray in their own words
15. **Worship** _(grade K-4, stage 1)_ — explain what worship is and describe ways Christians worship God individually and together
16. **Devotional Bible Reading** _(grade 3-7, stage 2)_ — describe a simple habit of daily Bible reading and reflection and explain why it helps a believer grow in faith
17. **Spiritual Disciplines** _(grade 2-6, stage 2)_ — name the core spiritual disciplines (prayer, worship, Bible reading) and explain why each helps a believer grow
18. **The Fruit of the Spirit** _(grade 2-6, stage 2)_ — list the fruit of the Spirit from Galatians 5 and give an everyday example of showing one of them
19. **Applying Scripture** _(grade 4-9, stage 3)_ — take a biblical principle from a passage and describe a specific way to apply it to everyday life
20. **Stewardship & Service** _(grade 4-9, stage 3)_ — explain that God calls people to use their time, talents, and resources to serve Him and others, and describe a way to serve
21. **Discipleship** _(grade 7-12, stage 4)_ — explain what it means to follow Jesus as a disciple and describe the practices and commitments of a growing Christian life
22. **Ethics & Moral Reasoning** _(grade 8-12, stage 4)_ — apply biblical principles to evaluate a moral situation and explain the reasoning behind a right course of action
23. **Faith in Action** _(grade 9-12, stage 5)_ — plan and carry out a way to live out their faith through service, witness, or generosity and reflect on its impact

### Church History
24. **Church History** _(grade 6-10, stage 3)_ — trace the growth of the Church from the early Church through key events such as the councils, the Reformation, and modern missions

### Doctrine & Theology
25. **Basic Biblical Themes** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — identify major biblical themes such as God's love, creation, sin, redemption, and covenant, and give an example of each from a story
26. **The Gospel & Salvation** _(grade 2-6, stage 2)_ — explain the gospel: that Jesus died and rose to save people from sin, and that salvation is received by grace through faith in Him
27. **Attributes of God** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — name and explain attributes of God such as holy, loving, all-powerful, all-knowing, and eternal
28. **Doctrinal Basics** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — explain core Christian beliefs about God, Jesus, the Bible, salvation, and the Church in their own words
29. **Sin & Grace** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — explain what sin is, that all people have sinned, and how God's grace offers forgiveness through Jesus
30. **The Holy Spirit** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — explain who the Holy Spirit is and describe His work in helping, guiding, and empowering believers
31. **The Trinity** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — explain that Christians believe in one God in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit
32. **The Second Coming** _(grade 7-11, stage 4)_ — explain the Christian hope that Jesus will return, and describe in broad terms what Scripture teaches about His second coming
33. **Theological Reasoning** _(grade 8-12, stage 4)_ — build a reasoned explanation of a doctrine by drawing on multiple Scripture passages and identifying how they support one another
34. **Modern Theology** _(grade 10-12, stage 5)_ — describe major movements and questions in modern theology and evaluate them in light of Scripture

### Key Figures & Events
35. **Key Bible Stories** _(grade K-3, stage 1)_ — retell several well-known Bible stories and identify the main person and lesson in each
36. **Daniel in Exile** _(grade 3-6, stage 2)_ — describe who Daniel was and retell a key event, such as Daniel in the lions' den or his stand for faithfulness in Babylon
37. **Elijah the Prophet** _(grade 3-6, stage 2)_ — describe who Elijah was and retell a key event from his life, such as the contest with the prophets of Baal on Mount Carmel
38. **Mary, Mother of Jesus** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — describe who Mary was and explain her role in the birth of Jesus
39. **Peter the Apostle** _(grade 3-7, stage 2)_ — describe who Peter was as one of Jesus' disciples and retell a key event from his life in the Gospels or in Acts
40. **Key Bible Events Timeline** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — arrange the major events of the Bible in chronological order from Creation to the early Church

### New Testament
41. **The Death & Resurrection of Jesus** _(grade 2-6, stage 2)_ — retell the events of Jesus' crucifixion and resurrection and explain that Christians celebrate the resurrection at Easter
42. **The Life of Jesus** _(grade 1-4, stage 2)_ — outline the main events of Jesus' life: His birth, baptism, ministry, death, and resurrection
43. **The Miracles of Jesus** _(grade 2-6, stage 2)_ — describe several miracles of Jesus and explain what they showed about His identity and compassion
44. **New Testament Survey** _(grade 6-10, stage 3)_ — place the major New Testament events in order from the Gospels through Acts, the Epistles, and Revelation and explain how they connect
45. **Paul's Missionary Journeys & Letters** _(grade 6-10, stage 3)_ — explain who Paul was, describe his missionary journeys, and identify several letters (epistles) he wrote to the churches
46. **The Early Church (Acts)** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — describe how the Church began at Pentecost with the coming of the Holy Spirit and how the first Christians lived and spread the gospel
47. **The Four Gospels** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — name the four Gospels (Matthew, Mark, Luke, John) and explain that each tells the account of Jesus' life and ministry
48. **The Parables of Jesus** _(grade 3-7, stage 3)_ — retell a parable of Jesus (e.g., the Good Samaritan or the Prodigal Son) and explain the lesson it teaches
49. **The Book of Revelation** _(grade 8-12, stage 4)_ — explain that Revelation is apocalyptic writing about the return of Christ and God's final victory, and describe its central hope of a new heaven and new earth

### Old Testament
50. **Creation & the Fall** _(grade K-3, stage 1)_ — retell the account of Creation and the Fall in Genesis and explain that sin entered the world through Adam and Eve's disobedience
51. **Noah & the Flood** _(grade K-3, stage 1)_ — retell the story of Noah and the Flood and explain the meaning of God's covenant sign of the rainbow
52. **Abraham & the Patriarchs** _(grade 1-4, stage 2)_ — describe God's covenant with Abraham and identify Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph as the patriarchs who followed him
53. **Israel's Kings: Saul, David & Solomon** _(grade 3-6, stage 2)_ — identify Saul, David, and Solomon as Israel's first kings and describe a key event from each reign
54. **Moses & the Exodus** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — retell how God used Moses to lead Israel out of slavery in Egypt, including the plagues, the Passover, and the crossing of the Red Sea
55. **The Psalms & Proverbs** _(grade 3-7, stage 2)_ — explain that Psalms are songs and prayers and Proverbs are short sayings of wisdom, and identify an example of each
56. **The Ten Commandments** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — list the Ten Commandments and explain that God gave them to Israel at Mount Sinai as a guide for living
57. **Old Testament Survey** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — place the major Old Testament events in order from Creation to the return from exile and explain how they form one unfolding story
58. **The Exile & Return** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — explain why Israel and Judah went into exile, name Babylon as the empire that conquered Judah, and describe the return to rebuild Jerusalem
59. **The Prophets** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — explain what a prophet did, name several major and minor prophets, and describe the kind of message prophets brought to Israel
