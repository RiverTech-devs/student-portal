# Art Studio (Creative Arts) — Lessons to Review (114)

All 114 lessons are newly AI-authored (Creative Arts had no game before). Scoped to visual/digital art. Each is a concept lesson (steps + worked example + misconceptions) with a knowledge/recognition multiple-choice generator. Review for accuracy (color theory, art history dates/movements, media properties, typography terms) and grade-appropriateness. Note: hands-on skills are assessed as recognition/knowledge questions, not production.

### Animation
  1. **2D Animation** _(grade 7-10, stage 2)_ — identify keyframes, in-betweens, and timing in a 2D animation
  2. **Animation Basics** _(grade 6-9, stage 2)_ — identify how a sequence of frames creates the illusion of motion (persistence of vision)
  3. **Character Animation** _(grade 8-11, stage 2)_ — identify the twelve principles of animation applied to character movement
  4. **Motion Graphics** _(grade 8-11, stage 2)_ — identify how animated typography and shapes communicate in motion graphics
  5. **VFX** _(grade 9-12, stage 2)_ — identify common visual-effects elements (particles, simulation, keying) in a shot
  6. **3D Basics** _(grade 7-10, stage 3)_ — identify core 3D concepts (mesh, vertex, camera, lighting) in a 3D scene
  7. **Rigging** _(grade 8-11, stage 3)_ — identify the purpose of a skeleton, joints, and controls in a character rig
  8. **Stop Motion** _(grade 5-8, stage 3)_ — explain how incremental physical movements captured frame-by-frame create stop motion
  9. **Compositing** _(grade 9-12, stage 4)_ — identify how layers, mattes, and color grading combine elements in a final composite
