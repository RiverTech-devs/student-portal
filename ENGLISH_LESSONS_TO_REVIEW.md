# English Lyceum — Lessons to Review

## A. New lessons (40) — full review needed

AI-authored (concept steps + worked example + misconceptions) with a multiple-choice generator each. Review for correctness, grade-appropriateness, clarity.

### Stage 1 — Foundations (K-1)
 1. **Phonological Awareness** _(grade K, Reading Foundations)_ — identify rhyming words, count syllables, and blend and segment spoken words into onsets and rimes
 2. **Print Concepts** _(grade K, Reading Foundations)_ — hold a book right-side up, track print left-to-right and top-to-bottom, and point to individual words while reading
 3. **Spelling** _(grade 1-2, Grammar and Conventions)_ — spell grade-level words correctly by applying phonics patterns, common spelling rules, and high-frequency word knowledge

### Stage 2 — Fluency (2-4)
 4. **Adjectives & Adverbs** _(grade 2-5, Grammar and Conventions)_ — identify adjectives and adverbs and explain which word each modifies
 5. **Capitalization** _(grade 2-4, Grammar and Conventions)_ — capitalize sentence beginnings, proper nouns, titles, and the pronoun I correctly
 6. **End Marks** _(grade 2-3, Grammar and Conventions)_ — end sentences with the correct period, question mark, or exclamation point
 7. **Nouns & Pronouns** _(grade 2-4, Grammar and Conventions)_ — identify common, proper, singular, and plural nouns and the pronouns that replace them
 8. **Subject & Predicate** _(grade 2-4, Grammar and Conventions)_ — identify the complete subject and complete predicate of a sentence
 9. **Verbs & Verbals** _(grade 2-5, Grammar and Conventions)_ — identify action, linking, and helping verbs and recognize verb forms in a sentence

### Stage 3 — Application (4-8)
10. **Apostrophes** _(grade 3-5, Grammar and Conventions)_ — use apostrophes correctly for contractions and possessives
11. **Commas** _(grade 3-6, Grammar and Conventions)_ — use commas correctly in series, compound sentences, addresses, and after introductory elements
12. **Commonly-Confused Words** _(grade 3-6, Grammar and Conventions)_ — correctly use commonly-confused words such as their/there/they're, your/you're, and its/it's
13. **Conjunctions & Prepositions** _(grade 3-5, Grammar and Conventions)_ — identify prepositions, coordinating and subordinating conjunctions, and interjections and use them correctly
14. **Denotation & Connotation** _(grade 4-7, Vocabulary)_ — distinguish a word's literal denotation from its positive, negative, or neutral connotation
15. **Dialogue & Quotation Punctuation** _(grade 4-7, Grammar and Conventions)_ — punctuate dialogue and quotations correctly with quotation marks, commas, and end marks
16. **Drafting** _(grade 3-6, Writing)_ — turn a prewriting plan into a complete first draft
17. **Editing & Proofreading** _(grade 4-7, Writing)_ — edit a draft to correct grammar, spelling, punctuation, and mechanics errors
18. **Figurative Language** _(grade 3-7, Literary Analysis)_ — identify and interpret similes, metaphors, personification, and hyperbole in a text
19. **Fragments & Run-ons** _(grade 3-6, Grammar and Conventions)_ — identify and correct sentence fragments and run-on sentences
20. **Idioms & Adages** _(grade 3-6, Vocabulary)_ — interpret the figurative meaning of common idioms, adages, and proverbs
21. **Multiple-Meaning Words** _(grade 3-5, Vocabulary)_ — distinguish among the meanings of a multiple-meaning word and choose the sense that fits the context
22. **Opinion Writing** _(grade 3-5, Writing)_ — write an opinion piece that states a preference, supplies reasons, and provides a concluding statement
23. **Point of View** _(grade 4-7, Literary Analysis)_ — identify the narrative point of view (first person, third-person limited, omniscient) and explain how it shapes a story
24. **Prewriting** _(grade 3-6, Writing)_ — generate and organize ideas before drafting using brainstorming, outlining, or graphic organizers
25. **Pronoun Usage & Agreement** _(grade 3-6, Grammar and Conventions)_ — use pronouns correctly and make them agree with their antecedents in number and gender
26. **Publishing** _(grade 4-7, Writing)_ — produce a clean, formatted final copy of a piece for an audience
27. **Reference & Dictionary Skills** _(grade 3-5, Vocabulary)_ — use a dictionary, glossary, or thesaurus to find a word's pronunciation, meaning, and part of speech
28. **Revising** _(grade 4-7, Writing)_ — revise a draft to improve ideas, organization, word choice, and clarity
29. **Sentence Types** _(grade 3-5, Grammar and Conventions)_ — identify and punctuate declarative, interrogative, imperative, and exclamatory sentences
30. **Setting & Conflict** _(grade 3-6, Literary Analysis)_ — describe a story's setting and identify its central conflict (character vs. self, others, society, or nature)
31. **Simple, Compound & Complex Sentences** _(grade 3-6, Grammar and Conventions)_ — distinguish and produce simple, compound, and complex sentences
32. **Subject-Verb Agreement** _(grade 3-6, Grammar and Conventions)_ — make verbs agree with their subjects in number, including with tricky subjects
33. **Verb Tense & Consistency** _(grade 3-6, Grammar and Conventions)_ — use verb tenses correctly and keep tense consistent within a piece of writing
34. **Word Relationships** _(grade 3-5, Vocabulary)_ — identify synonyms, antonyms, homophones, and complete simple word analogies

### Stage 4 — Integration (7-10)
35. **Claims & Counterclaims** _(grade 6-10, Language Study)_ — distinguish a claim from a counterclaim and acknowledge and refute opposing views
36. **Dialect** _(grade 6-College, Language Study)_ — recognize regional and social dialects and explain how variety in language reflects audience and context
37. **Evaluate an Argument** _(grade 6-9, Advanced Reading)_ — evaluate the claims, evidence, and reasoning in a text and judge whether the argument is sound
38. **Evidence & Reasoning** _(grade 6-10, Language Study)_ — select relevant evidence and explain the reasoning that links it to a claim
39. **Literary Devices** _(grade 6-10, Literary Analysis)_ — identify literary devices such as symbolism, irony, foreshadowing, and allusion and explain their effect
40. **Register (Formal & Informal)** _(grade 5-9, Speaking and Listening)_ — choose an appropriate level of formality in language for a given audience, purpose, and situation
