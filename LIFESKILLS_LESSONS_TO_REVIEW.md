# Life Compass (Life Skills) — Lessons to Review (63)

All 63 lessons are newly AI-authored (Life Skills had no game before). Each is a concept lesson (steps + worked example + misconceptions) with a knowledge/decision-scenario multiple-choice generator. Review for accuracy and age-appropriateness — especially the financial (interest/taxes/credit), health/first-aid/safety, and any advice-bearing content.

### Career & Work Readiness
 1. **Work Habits** _(grade 3-7, stage 2)_ — demonstrate reliable work habits such as being on time, following directions, and finishing tasks
 2. **Career Exploration** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — research several careers, describe what each involves, and connect them to their own interests and strengths
 3. **Entrepreneurship** _(grade 9-12, stage 4)_ — develop a simple business idea, describe its customers and costs, and explain how it would make a profit
 4. **Interview Skills** _(grade 8-12, stage 4)_ — prepare for a job interview, answer common questions clearly, and present themselves professionally
 5. **Job Readiness** _(grade 8-12, stage 4)_ — complete a job application and describe the steps of finding, applying for, and starting a job
 6. **Networking** _(grade 9-12, stage 4)_ — explain how professional relationships lead to opportunities and describe how to build and maintain a network
 7. **Resume Basics** _(grade 8-12, stage 4)_ — write a basic resume that lists their skills, experience, and education in a clear, organized format
 8. **Workplace Etiquette** _(grade 8-12, stage 4)_ — describe professional behavior on the job, including punctuality, dress, communication, and respect for coworkers

### Digital & Ethics
 9. **Digital Citizenship & Online Safety** _(grade 3-8, stage 2)_ — describe safe, respectful online behavior and explain how to protect privacy, passwords, and a positive digital footprint
10. **Digital Productivity** _(grade 4-9, stage 3)_ — use common digital tools (documents, email, calendars, search) to organize information and complete tasks
11. **Ethics & Integrity** _(grade 4-9, stage 3)_ — explain what it means to act with honesty and integrity and evaluate a situation for the right thing to do

### Financial Literacy
12. **Needs vs Wants** _(grade K-2, stage 1)_ — distinguish between needs and wants and sort everyday examples of each
13. **Basic Money Concepts** _(grade 1-4, stage 2)_ — identify coins and bills, state their values, and make change for a small purchase
14. **Saving vs Spending** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — explain the difference between saving and spending and describe why setting money aside for a goal helps
15. **Avoiding Scams & Consumer Awareness** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — recognize common scams and deceptive practices and describe how to protect their money and personal information
16. **Banking & Transactions** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — explain the purpose of a bank and describe how deposits, withdrawals, and transfers work
17. **Budgeting** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — build a simple budget that balances income against expenses and explain why tracking spending matters
18. **Checking & Savings Accounts** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — explain the difference between a checking and a savings account and describe how to open and use each
19. **Comparison Shopping** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — compare price, quantity, and quality of similar products and identify the best value
20. **Debit & Credit Cards** _(grade 6-10, stage 3)_ — explain the difference between a debit and a credit card and describe the responsibilities of using each
21. **Interest** _(grade 6-10, stage 3)_ — explain how interest is earned on savings and charged on loans and calculate a simple interest amount
22. **Online Banking & Digital Payments** _(grade 6-10, stage 3)_ — describe how to bank online, send a digital payment, and keep account access secure
23. **Credit & Debt** _(grade 7-11, stage 4)_ — explain how credit works, what debt is, and how a credit score is affected by borrowing and repayment
24. **Insurance Basics** _(grade 8-12, stage 4)_ — explain how insurance spreads risk and describe common types such as health, auto, and renters insurance
25. **Investing Basics** _(grade 9-12, stage 4)_ — explain how investing grows money over time and describe the trade-off between risk and return
26. **Paychecks & Deductions** _(grade 7-11, stage 4)_ — read a pay stub and explain the difference between gross and net pay and common deductions
27. **Taxes Basics** _(grade 8-12, stage 4)_ — explain why people pay taxes, name common types of taxes, and describe what filing a tax return involves
28. **Financial Independence** _(grade 10-12, stage 5)_ — outline a plan to support themselves financially, covering income, budgeting, saving, credit, and taxes

### Health & Wellness
29. **Sleep & Hygiene** _(grade K-3, stage 1)_ — describe healthy sleep and personal hygiene routines and explain why each keeps the body well
30. **Nutrition & Healthy Eating** _(grade 2-6, stage 2)_ — identify the major food groups and plan a balanced meal that supports good health
31. **Physical Fitness** _(grade 2-6, stage 2)_ — explain why regular exercise matters and describe activities that build strength, endurance, and flexibility
32. **First Aid & Safety Basics** _(grade 5-10, stage 3)_ — respond to a common minor injury and describe basic safety and emergency procedures, including when to call for help
33. **Health & Wellness** _(grade 4-9, stage 3)_ — explain how nutrition, exercise, sleep, and hygiene work together to keep the body and mind healthy
34. **Mental Health & Stress Management** _(grade 5-10, stage 3)_ — recognize signs of stress and describe healthy strategies for managing emotions and when to seek help
35. **Substance-Abuse Awareness** _(grade 6-10, stage 3)_ — describe the risks of alcohol, tobacco, and drug use and identify strategies for refusing and getting help

### Independent Living
36. **Laundry** _(grade 3-7, stage 2)_ — sort, wash, dry, and fold clothing correctly and read a garment care label
37. **Cleaning & Home Maintenance** _(grade 6-10, stage 3)_ — carry out routine cleaning and describe basic home maintenance tasks and when to call for repairs
38. **Cooking Basics** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — follow a simple recipe safely, using basic kitchen tools and safe food-handling practices
39. **Grocery Shopping** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — make a shopping list, find items in a store, and stay within a budget while comparing prices
40. **Home Management** _(grade 7-11, stage 3)_ — plan and carry out the routine tasks of running a household, including cleaning, upkeep, and supplies
41. **Independent Living Basics** _(grade 6-10, stage 3)_ — describe the everyday self-care and household tasks needed to live independently and explain why each matters
42. **Transportation & Navigation** _(grade 6-10, stage 3)_ — plan a route using public transit or maps and describe safe, reliable ways to get where they need to go
43. **Meal Planning** _(grade 7-11, stage 4)_ — plan a week of balanced meals and build a shopping list that fits a set budget
44. **Renting & Housing Basics** _(grade 9-12, stage 4)_ — explain how renting works, read the key terms of a lease, and describe the costs of housing
45. **Adult Responsibility** _(grade 11-12, stage 5)_ — describe the core responsibilities of adult life and demonstrate the ability to manage them independently

### Personal Development
46. **Growth Mindset** _(grade K-3, stage 1)_ — explain that ability grows with effort and give an example of learning from a mistake instead of giving up
47. **Personal Responsibility** _(grade 1-3, stage 1)_ — explain what it means to be responsible and give examples of owning their actions, chores, and mistakes
48. **Self-Awareness** _(grade K-2, stage 1)_ — identify their own emotions, strengths, and areas for growth and explain how feelings affect their choices
49. **Goal Setting** _(grade 3-6, stage 2)_ — set a specific, measurable goal and list the steps and timeline needed to reach it
50. **Organization** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — keep their materials, files, and space in order and explain a system for finding what they need
51. **Time Management** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — plan a simple schedule, prioritize tasks, and explain how to use a calendar or to-do list to meet a deadline
52. **Decision Making** _(grade 4-7, stage 3)_ — weigh options against pros, cons, and consequences and explain the reasoning behind a choice
53. **Problem Solving** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — define a problem, generate several solutions, test one, and evaluate whether it worked
54. **Resilience** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — describe strategies for coping with setbacks and explain how to bounce back and keep trying after a failure
55. **Self-Advocacy** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — identify their own needs and ask for help, accommodations, or resources in an appropriate way
56. **Long-Term Planning** _(grade 8-11, stage 4)_ — build a multi-year plan toward a major life goal and identify the milestones and resources required

### Social & Communication Skills
57. **Active Listening** _(grade K-3, stage 1)_ — listen without interrupting, restate what a speaker said, and ask a relevant follow-up question
58. **Collaboration** _(grade 3-6, stage 2)_ — work with others toward a shared goal, contribute a fair share, and describe each member's role
59. **Empathy** _(grade 1-4, stage 2)_ — recognize how another person feels and describe a caring, respectful way to respond
60. **Assertiveness** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — state their needs, opinions, and boundaries clearly and respectfully without being passive or aggressive
61. **Conflict Resolution** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — use calm, respectful strategies to resolve a disagreement and reach a compromise
62. **Giving & Receiving Feedback** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — give specific, kind, useful feedback and accept feedback without becoming defensive
63. **Professional Communication** _(grade 7-10, stage 4)_ — write a clear professional email and speak appropriately in a formal or workplace setting
