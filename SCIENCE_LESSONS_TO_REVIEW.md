# Science Lab — Lessons to Review (79)

All 79 lessons are newly AI-authored (Science had no game before). Each is a concept lesson (steps + worked example + misconceptions) with a randomized multiple-choice generator. Review for scientific accuracy, grade-appropriateness, and clarity. Highest-scrutiny: the advanced/abstract topics (genetics, DNA, evolution, chemical bonding, Newton laws, waves, climate) where distractors and worked examples are easiest to get subtly wrong.

### Scientific Practices
 1. **Basic Experimentation** _(grade 1-3, stage 1)_ — carry out a simple hands-on experiment following given steps and report what happened
 2. **Observation Skills** _(grade K-2, stage 1)_ — make and describe detailed qualitative observations using their senses and simple tools
 3. **Questioning & Curiosity** _(grade K-2, stage 1)_ — ask a testable question about something they observe in the natural world
 4. **Data Collection** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — gather measurements and observations during an investigation and record them systematically
 5. **Data Recording** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — organize collected data into a labeled table or chart with correct units
 6. **Hypothesis Formation** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — state a testable, falsifiable hypothesis as an if-then prediction before an experiment
 7. **Measurement & Tools** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — choose and use appropriate tools and standard units to measure length, mass, volume, temperature, and time
 8. **Scientific Method** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — list and order the steps of the scientific method and apply them to a simple guided investigation
 9. **Variables & Controls** _(grade 4-6, stage 2)_ — identify the independent, dependent, and controlled variables in an experiment
10. **Argument from Evidence** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — make a scientific claim and support or challenge it using relevant evidence and reasoning
11. **Data Analysis (Basic)** _(grade 5-7, stage 3)_ — read a data table or graph and describe the pattern or relationship it shows
12. **Scientific Communication** _(grade 5-8, stage 3)_ — communicate findings clearly using scientific vocabulary, tables, and diagrams
13. **Scientific Models** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — build and use a diagram, physical, or conceptual model to represent a system or process
14. **Systems Thinking** _(grade 6-8, stage 3)_ — describe a system in terms of its components, inputs, outputs, and interactions
15. **Data Analysis (Advanced)** _(grade 8-10, stage 4)_ — analyze data for trends, error, and uncertainty and judge whether it supports a claim
16. **Lab Design** _(grade 7-9, stage 4)_ — design a controlled investigation that tests a hypothesis with an appropriate procedure and controls
17. **Mathematical Modeling** _(grade 8-11, stage 4)_ — use equations, rates, and proportional reasoning to model a scientific relationship quantitatively
18. **Theory & Explanation** _(grade 8-11, stage 4)_ — construct an evidence-based explanation that connects observations to underlying scientific theory
19. **Independent Investigation** _(grade 9-12, stage 5)_ — independently plan, conduct, and document an original scientific investigation end to end
20. **Scientific Mastery Project** _(grade 9-12, stage 5)_ — produce and defend a complete research project with question, method, data, analysis, and conclusion

### Engineering
21. **Defining Problems** _(grade 3-6, stage 2)_ — define a simple design problem including its criteria for success and constraints
22. **Engineering Design Process** _(grade 3-6, stage 2)_ — follow the engineering design cycle to define, plan, build, test, and improve a solution
23. **Testing Solutions** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — build and fairly test a prototype, compare it to criteria, and use results to improve it

### Physical Science
24. **Properties of Matter** _(grade K-2, stage 1)_ — describe and sort objects by observable material properties such as color, texture, hardness, and flexibility
25. **Energy Types** _(grade 3-5, stage 2)_ — identify forms of energy (kinetic, thermal, light, sound, electrical) and give examples of energy transfer
26. **Mixtures & Solutions** _(grade 3-5, stage 2)_ — distinguish a mixture from a solution and describe how components can be separated
27. **States of Matter** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — identify solids, liquids, and gases and describe how heating and cooling change state
28. **Thermal Energy** _(grade 5-8, stage 3)_ — explain heat transfer by conduction, convection, and radiation and predict temperature change

### Chemistry
29. **Atoms and Molecules** _(grade 5-8, stage 3)_ — describe the structure of an atom (protons, neutrons, electrons) and how atoms combine into molecules
30. **Basic Chemistry** _(grade 5-8, stage 3)_ — classify substances as elements, compounds, or mixtures and read a simple chemical formula
31. **Chemistry (Intro)** _(grade 5-8, stage 3)_ — describe matter as made of particles and explain physical versus chemical properties
32. **Conservation of Matter** _(grade 5-8, stage 3)_ — explain that matter is conserved in physical and chemical changes and mass does not disappear
33. **Periodic Table** _(grade 6-9, stage 3)_ — use the periodic table to find an element's symbol, atomic number, and group and period
34. **Chemical Bonding** _(grade 9-12, stage 4)_ — distinguish ionic from covalent bonds and explain why atoms bond
35. **Chemical Reactions** _(grade 6-9, stage 4)_ — identify evidence of a chemical reaction and describe how new substances are formed
36. **Chemistry Reactions** _(grade 7-10, stage 4)_ — interpret a balanced chemical equation and identify reactants and products
37. **Advanced Chemistry** _(grade 9-12, stage 5)_ — apply stoichiometry, bonding, and reaction types to solve multi-step chemistry problems

### Physics
38. **Magnetism** _(grade 3-6, stage 2)_ — describe magnetic poles, attraction and repulsion, and identify magnetic materials
39. **Simple Machines** _(grade 3-6, stage 2)_ — identify the six simple machines and explain how each changes force or motion to do work
40. **Electricity & Circuits** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — build a simple circuit and explain the roles of a source, conductor, switch, and load
41. **Forces and Motion** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — identify forces acting on an object and predict how balanced and unbalanced forces change its motion
42. **Gravity** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — explain gravity as an attractive force between masses and describe its effect on falling objects and orbits
43. **Light** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — explain how light travels, reflects, and refracts and how we see objects and colors
44. **Physics (Intro)** _(grade 5-8, stage 3)_ — describe motion using position, speed, and direction and represent it on a graph
45. **Sound** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — explain that sound is produced by vibrations and relate pitch and volume to frequency and amplitude
46. **Waves** _(grade 6-9, stage 3)_ — describe a wave by its amplitude, wavelength, and frequency and how it transfers energy
47. **Newton's Laws of Motion** _(grade 8-11, stage 4)_ — state and apply Newton's three laws of motion to explain everyday phenomena
48. **Physics Systems** _(grade 8-11, stage 4)_ — analyze energy transfer and conservation within a physical system
49. **Advanced Physics** _(grade 9-12, stage 5)_ — apply quantitative laws of motion, energy, and forces to solve multi-step physics problems

### Life Science
50. **Animal Biology** _(grade K-3, stage 1)_ — describe basic animal needs, life cycles, and how animals are grouped by shared features
51. **Human Body** _(grade K-4, stage 1)_ — name major external and internal body parts and describe their basic functions
52. **Biology (Intro)** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — distinguish living from non-living things and describe the basic needs of organisms
53. **Ecosystems** _(grade 3-6, stage 2)_ — describe how organisms in an ecosystem interact and depend on their environment
54. **Food Webs** _(grade 3-6, stage 2)_ — trace the flow of energy through a food chain and food web from producers to consumers to decomposers
55. **Plant Biology** _(grade 1-4, stage 2)_ — name the parts of a plant and describe what plants need to grow and reproduce
56. **Biology Systems** _(grade 6-8, stage 3)_ — explain how structures and processes in an organism function as interacting systems
57. **Body Systems** _(grade 5-8, stage 3)_ — describe the major human body systems and how they work together to keep the body alive
58. **Cells** _(grade 5-8, stage 3)_ — identify a cell as the basic unit of life and label major parts of plant and animal cells
59. **Microorganisms** _(grade 5-8, stage 3)_ — describe bacteria, viruses, and fungi and explain their helpful and harmful roles
60. **Photosynthesis & Respiration** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — explain how plants make food by photosynthesis and how cells release energy through respiration
61. **Advanced Biology** _(grade 9-12, stage 4)_ — analyze complex biological processes and connect molecular, cellular, and ecosystem levels
62. **DNA** _(grade 8-11, stage 4)_ — describe DNA as the molecule that stores genetic information and how genes code for traits
63. **Evolution & Natural Selection** _(grade 8-11, stage 4)_ — explain how natural selection acts on variation to drive evolution over generations
64. **Heredity & Genetics** _(grade 7-10, stage 4)_ — use dominant and recessive traits and a Punnett square to predict inheritance

### Earth Science
65. **Earth Science** _(grade 2-5, stage 2)_ — describe Earth's major materials and features and the basic land, water, and air systems
66. **Rock Cycle** _(grade 3-6, stage 2)_ — classify rocks as igneous, sedimentary, or metamorphic and describe how the rock cycle transforms them
67. **Water Cycle** _(grade 3-6, stage 2)_ — describe evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and collection in the water cycle
68. **Weather Patterns** _(grade 3-6, stage 2)_ — read weather instruments and describe how temperature, air pressure, and moisture create weather
69. **Earth's Systems** _(grade 5-8, stage 3)_ — describe how the geosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere, and biosphere interact
70. **Natural Resources** _(grade 4-8, stage 3)_ — distinguish renewable from non-renewable resources and explain conservation of Earth's resources
71. **Plate Tectonics** _(grade 6-9, stage 3)_ — explain how moving plates cause earthquakes, volcanoes, and mountain formation
72. **Earth History** _(grade 7-10, stage 4)_ — use rock layers and fossils to describe the relative age and history of Earth

### Space Science
73. **Moon Phases & Seasons** _(grade 3-6, stage 2)_ — explain the cause of moon phases and of seasons using Earth's tilt and orbit
74. **Solar System** _(grade 2-6, stage 2)_ — name the planets in order and describe the Sun, Moon, and their motions in the solar system
75. **Galaxies & Universe** _(grade 8-11, stage 4)_ — describe galaxies and the scale and expansion of the universe supported by evidence
76. **Space Science** _(grade 7-10, stage 4)_ — describe stars, galaxies, and gravity's role in the structure and motion of the universe

### Environmental Science
77. **Climate & Environment** _(grade 5-8, stage 3)_ — describe how living things and human activity interact with and affect the environment
78. **Climate Change** _(grade 5-9, stage 3)_ — explain the greenhouse effect and cite evidence for human-caused climate change and its impacts
79. **Weather vs Climate** _(grade 5-8, stage 3)_ — distinguish day-to-day weather from long-term climate using data over time
